## What UofA Already Gives You

| You need | Where you get it at UofA |
|---|---|
| The algorithms behind most coding rounds (sorting, graphs, DP, greedy) | [CMPUT 204](../courses/cmput-204.md). Review your 204 notes before you start grinding problems. |
| Solving timed problems from a written description, then implementing them | [CMPUT 303](../courses/cmput-303.md) or [CMPUT 403](../courses/cmput-403.md). Lectures are shared; 403 adds contest material and needs a 300-level CMPUT prerequisite. Students often call it the best interview prep at UofA; per the 403 course page, the instructor recommends 303 unless you already have a contest background. |
| Weekly practice with other people | [UAPSPC](https://uapspc.github.io/) meets weekly and fields ICPC teams; the Rocky Mountain Regional is each fall and UAPC is each March ([Clubs and Organizations](../community/clubs-and-orgs.md)). You don't have to compete to attend. |
| Behavioural stories | [CMPUT 301](../courses/cmput-301.md) (a term-long team app) and [CMPUT 401](../courses/cmput-401.md) (a team project for a real stakeholder) give you conflicts, deadlines, changing requirements, and client communication to talk about. Write the stories down when the term ends, while you still remember the details. |
| Reading and reviewing other people's code | [CMPUT 402](../courses/cmput-402.md) covers testing, reviews, and continuous integration. |
| Mock interviews and resume review | The [SIP office](https://22436.waitwell.ca/) (if you're in SIP) and [Career Centre advising](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/career-centre/programs-services/advising-appointments/index.html). |

## How Interviews Changed in 2025-26

The classic format (an algorithm problem on a video call) is under pressure from both sides: candidates using AI to cheat, and companies whose engineers now code with AI every day. Expect the format to vary by company, and **read each company's AI policy before every round**.

- **Some companies now expect AI in the interview.** Canva replaced its computer science fundamentals screen with an "AI-Assisted Coding" round for backend, frontend, and ML candidates: a realistic, ambiguous product problem where you're judged on decomposing it, deciding when to use the AI, and catching and fixing its mistakes ([Canva Engineering, June 2025](https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/yes-you-can-use-ai-in-our-interviews/)). Meta began building a coding interview in which candidates have an AI assistant ([404 Media, July 2025](https://www.404media.co/meta-is-going-to-let-job-candidates-use-ai-during-coding-tests/)).
- **Others forbid it.** Anthropic's candidate guidance allows AI for polishing applications and preparing, but not in live interviews or take-homes unless told otherwise ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/candidate-ai-guidance)).
- **In-person rounds are coming back.** After tools marketed for cheating in remote coding interviews spread, Google leadership discussed moving some interviews back on site ([CNBC, March 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/03/09/google-ai-interview-coder-cheat.html)), and Sundar Pichai later said Google was reintroducing at least one in-person round ([Entrepreneur, Aug 2025](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/google-mckinsey-reintroduce-in-person-interviews-due-to-ai/496041)). For remote students, that can mean travelling for a final round.
- **Interviewers probe more.** The same CNBC report describes interviewers watching for off-screen reading and asking candidates to explain how they reached an answer; Google's recruiting VP said interviewers are told to probe answers to check understanding. If you can't explain your code line by line, it doesn't matter who wrote it.

**What to do about it:** practise both ways. Solve problems unaided so you can pass a proctored or in-person round, and practise building something small with an AI assistant while reviewing every line it writes, so you can pass an AI-assisted one. How UofA courses treat AI is on [Learning and Working With AI](../skills/learning-with-ai.md).

## Generic Prep: Go Here Instead

- **[Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/):** free, end-to-end guide to coding, system design, and behavioural rounds.
