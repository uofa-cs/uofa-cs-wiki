# The Science Internship Program (SIP)

SIP is UofA's internship (co-op style) program for Faculty of Science students, including Computing Science. There is no separate CS co-op; SIP is it. You take one continuous block of 4 to 16 months off school to work full-time for a paid employer, then come back to finish your degree ([SIP overview](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/experiential-learning/opportunities/science-internship-program.html)).

**Our take:** if you're in the Software Practice Option, SIP isn't optional. For everyone else, it's worth it if you want a long placement (and the employers who only hire students enrolled in a formal internship program), and can live with graduating later and paying term fees while you work.

**Can you go out more than once?** The SIP FAQ says yes if your first internship was 4 or 8 months, up to 16 months total; once you've done 12 months you're no longer eligible ([FAQ](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/student-faq.html)). The [2026-27 Calendar](https://calendar.ualberta.ca/content.php?catoid=69&navoid=20749) words it as one 8-month (possibly two separate 4-month placements), 12-month or 16-month internship per degree. If you're planning a split placement, confirm with the SIP team first.

**Extensions:** if your employer offers to extend you and you're under 16 months, you may be able to accept; talk to a SIP coordinator ([Tuition and Courses](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/tuition-and-courses.html)).

## The Transcript Designation (8+ Months)

Complete at least 8 months and you get a "with internship" designation on your transcript and degree parchment. A single 4-month term shows up as one WKEXP course on your transcript but doesn't earn the designation ([Calendar](https://calendar.ualberta.ca/content.php?catoid=69&navoid=20749), [SIP Requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html)).

## How and When to Apply

Getting into SIP and getting a job are two separate steps.

1. **Join SIP.** Sign in to [campusBRIDGE](https://campusbridge.ualberta.ca/home.htm) with your CCID, pick the internship program (apply once only), upload your latest transcript, and wait for the SIP team to approve your profile ([SIP Requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html)). The intake windows are:

    | Opens | Closes |
    |---|---|
    | Beginning of September | End of September |
    | Beginning of January | End of January |
    | Early May | Early June |

2. **Apply to jobs.** Once approved you can see and apply to postings in campusBRIDGE. Each posting has its own deadline, and some want you to apply on the company's own site instead ([FAQ](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/student-faq.html)). You can also apply to jobs on public boards and bring them into SIP (see [Finding your own](#finding-your-own-placement) below).
3. **Accepting offers.** You must accept or decline an offer within 24 hours, and you can't decline more than one SIP-obtained offer per term without a justifiable reason; do it twice and you may lose campusBRIDGE access ([FAQ](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/student-faq.html)). Don't apply to postings you wouldn't take.
4. **After you accept:** create a work-term record in campusBRIDGE, upload your offer letter, and email sciiip@ualberta.ca. The SIP team registers you in your WKEXP course; you can't do it yourself ([FAQ](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/student-faq.html)).

The SIP office also does resume/cover letter reviews and mock interviews; [book an appointment](https://22436.waitwell.ca/).

### Suggested timeline

The Faculty's own [internship timeline](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/internship-timeline.html) recommends:

| Year | What to do |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | Attend a SIP info session, finish 24 units, apply to SIP at the end of the year |
| Year 2 | Get your resume reviewed; start applying for placements (you need 48 units by the time you start) |
| Year 3 | Keep applying |
| Year 4 | You need a full course load left when you return, so **Winter of Year 4 may be your last chance** to start |

## Fees: You Pay to Work

While on placement you're registered in one WKEXP course per 4-month term and are considered a full-time student ([Calendar](https://calendar.ualberta.ca/content.php?catoid=69&navoid=20749)). Those courses carry fees ([Tuition and Courses](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/tuition-and-courses.html)):

| Placement length | Courses | Fee index |
|---|---|---|
| 4 months | WKEXP 955 | 9 |
| 8 months | + WKEXP 956 | 9 |
| 12 months | + WKEXP 957 | 1 |
| 16 months | + WKEXP 958 | 1 |

The Registrar's [2026-27 sample assessment](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/registrar/costs-tuition-fees/undergraduate-tuition/open-studies-work-experience-audit-fees.html) for one Science work experience term (fee index 9) is **$1,094.04 tuition, $2,190.80 total** once non-instructional fees (SU, health and dental plans, U-Pass, etc.) are added. Terms 3 and 4 (fee index 1) carry about a ninth of that tuition, but the non-instructional fees still apply. Check the [cost calculator](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/registrar/costs-tuition-fees/index.html) or the [Student Service Centre](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/services/student-service-centre/index.html) for your own numbers, especially if you're an international student.

Two ways to save:

- **U-Pass opt-out:** if you're working remotely or outside the transit area (Edmonton, Strathcona, St. Albert, Leduc, Fort Saskatchewan, Spruce Grove, Beaumont), you may be exempt from the U-Pass fee; the form is on the [Tuition and Courses](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/tuition-and-courses.html) page.
- **One course on the side:** you may take one course per internship term alongside WKEXP if it's outside work hours, your supervisor approves, and you'll still have a full course load left when you return ([Tuition and Courses](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/tuition-and-courses.html)).

## Effect on Graduation

The Faculty says plainly that SIP "might add a year to a 4-year science degree" ([SIP Requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html)). An 8-month placement starting in May eats one Fall term; 12 or 16 months starting in May or September takes a full academic year. Plan it with your [degree planning](../getting-started/degree-planning.md) so you still have at least 3 courses (and any prerequisite chains, like the 400-level CMPUT courses you need) left for after.

---

## Mandatory for the Software Practice Option

Both the Honors and Major in Computing Science, Software Practice Option, require an **8-, 12- or 16-month SIP**. A 4-month placement doesn't satisfy it, and if you fail to complete a placement you must withdraw from the option and reapply to the regular Honors or Major ([2026-27 Calendar, Computing Science](https://calendar.ualberta.ca/preview_program.php?catoid=69&poid=110935)). If you're in Software Practice, apply to SIP as soon as you have 24 units. See the [program overview](../getting-started/program-overview.md) for how the options compare.

## International Students

- You're eligible for SIP, but some positions require Canadian citizenship ([SIP Requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html)).
- The SIP page says international students no longer need a co-op/intern work permit. Once you've secured a placement and told the SIP team, they give you a letter confirming your participation, which IRCC may ask for ([SIP Requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html)).
- This follows IRCC's change: as of April 1, 2026, post-secondary students don't need a co-op work permit for student work placements, but IRCC lists conditions (on-campus work conditions printed on your study permit, a DLI letter confirming the placement is required, full-time status, placement totalling 50% or less of your program) ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/intern.html)).

Immigration rules depend on your individual permit. Before you accept an offer, confirm your situation with [International Student and Visitor Services (ISVS)](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/international/international-student-services/contact-us/index.html).

## SIP vs. Finding a Summer Internship Yourself

You don't need SIP to work in the summer. A May to August internship falls in your normal break, so you can take one without enrolling in SIP, paying WKEXP fees, or delaying graduation. (International students: IRCC lets eligible students work unlimited off-campus hours during scheduled breaks such as summer, under the [off-campus work rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html) rather than SIP; confirm with ISVS.)

| | SIP | Summer internship on your own |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 4 to 16 months | Fits in the May to August break |
| Fees | WKEXP fees each term | None |
| Graduation | May add up to a year | No delay |
| Transcript | "With internship" designation at 8+ months | Nothing |
| Job access | campusBRIDGE postings, plus employers that only hire students in a formal co-op/internship program ([FAQ](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/student-faq.html)) | Public postings only |
| Support | SIP coordinator, resume/interview help, help if a placement goes wrong | You're on your own |

### Finding your own placement

You can land a job outside campusBRIDGE and run it through SIP, as long as it's at least 30 hours a week, paid at least minimum wage, has a supervisor knowledgeable in the field, and the work relates to your degree ([FAQ](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/student-faq.html)).

For how to find and land internships in general, see [Internships](internships.md).
