# Career Paths

A map from the path you're curious about to what UofA offers for it: the courses, the research groups and clubs, and the Edmonton employers in that space. What each job involves day to day is a search away; this page is about where to find it here.

Course links go to the wiki's course pages; check each one's prerequisites and recent terms before planning around it. Employers come from the verified [Edmonton employer directory](edmonton-tech-scene.md#employer-directory); "none verified" means we couldn't confirm local student hiring, not that nobody hires.

*Last verified: October 2026.*
