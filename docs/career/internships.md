# Internships

When to apply, where UofA students find postings, and what the market looks like right now. For the rules of the Faculty's internship program, see the [Science Internship Program (SIP)](science-internship-program.md); for local employers, see [Edmonton's Tech Scene](edmonton-tech-scene.md).

*Last verified: October 2026.*

## When the Cycle Runs

Summer internships are mostly filled the fall before. If you want a May start, your applications should be going out in September and October.

| When | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| September | SIP intake opens (closes end of September). Careers Day, UofA's biggest fair (Sept 23, 2026). TELUS posts its Engineering and Technology co-op roles for the following winter and summer. | [SIP requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html), [Career fairs](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/career-centre/connect-with-employers/career-fairs/index.html), [TELUS co-op](https://www.telus.com/en/about/careers/students-grads/co-op) |
| October | Large companies close summer applications early: Shopify's Summer 2027 engineering and data internships close October 12, 2026. | [Shopify internships](https://internships.shopify.com/) |
| January to March | Second SIP intake (January). Spring Hiring Fair (Feb 9, 2027). The Career Centre's Tech Industry Career Mixer with local tech employers (March 3 in 2026). TELUS standard summer co-op postings go up around February. Paid summer research (USRA, URI) applications are due in early February ([details](../research/getting-into-research.md)). | [SIP requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html), [Career fairs](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/career-centre/connect-with-employers/career-fairs/index.html), [Tech mixer](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/career-centre/connect-with-employers/tech-mixer-for-students.html), [TELUS co-op](https://www.telus.com/en/about/careers/students-grads/co-op) |
| April to June | Government of Alberta summer student call (posted in April in 2026). Third SIP intake (early May to early June). TELUS posts fall co-op terms in May. | [GoA student jobs](https://www.alberta.ca/find-student-opportunities-and-internships), [SIP requirements](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/science/student-services/internship-careers/student-interns/sip-program-requirements.html) |
| Year-round | The federal student hiring inventory (FSWEP) is open all year. Smaller and local companies post whenever they need someone. | [FSWEP](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-service-commission/jobs/services/recruitment/students/federal-student-work-program.html) |

**Our take:** treat September to October as the main window for summer roles at large companies, and keep applying through the winter for local, government, and SIP postings, which run on a later and less predictable schedule.

## SIP or On Your Own?

You don't have to choose one. SIP gives you 4 to 16 month placements, campusBRIDGE postings, and employers that only hire students in a formal program, at the cost of WKEXP fees and possibly a later graduation. A May to August internship you find yourself fits in your summer break with no fees and no delay. The full comparison is on the [SIP page](science-internship-program.md#sip-vs-finding-a-summer-internship-yourself). If you're in the Software Practice Option, SIP is mandatory.

## Where UofA Students Find Postings

- **[campusBRIDGE](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/career-centre/campusbridge.html):** UofA's job portal. Every student can use the Students and Alumni job board with their CCID and set up saved searches and notifications. Once you're approved for SIP, the same portal shows SIP postings.
- **Company career pages:** most tech employers hire through their own sites. The [Edmonton employer directory](edmonton-tech-scene.md#employer-directory) links each one; set up alerts on the ones you care about.
- **[Taproot Edmonton's tech roundup](https://edmonton.taproot.news/roundups/tech):** a weekly list of local tech openings, useful for smaller companies that never post on campus.
- **Government:** [FSWEP](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-service-commission/jobs/services/recruitment/students/federal-student-work-program.html) (federal) and the [Government of Alberta](https://www.alberta.ca/find-student-opportunities-and-internships) student board.
- **Career fairs and mixers:** Careers Day, the Spring Hiring Fair, and the Tech Industry Career Mixer, all registered through campusBRIDGE. Dates are in the [events calendar](../community/clubs-and-orgs.md#annual-events-calendar).
- **The CompE Club's [resources repo](https://github.com/UAlbertaCompEClub/CompE_Resources)** collects internship boards and interview prep links, and its Discord does resume reviews ([Clubs and Organizations](../community/clubs-and-orgs.md)).
- **Research jobs:** paid summer research (USRA and similar) is a real alternative to an industry internship; see [Getting Into Research](../research/getting-into-research.md).

## The 2025-26 Market, Honestly

It got harder, then eased a little. The numbers:

- **Student summer jobs in Canada:** returning students averaged **17.9% unemployment** in summer 2025, the highest since 2009 apart from 2020 ([Statistics Canada, Sept 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/250905/dq250905a-eng.htm)). Summer 2026 improved to **15.9%**, and to 9.2% for returning students aged 20 to 24 (down from 12.3%) ([Statistics Canada, Sept 2026](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260904/dq260904a-eng.htm)).
- **Early-20s jobs in Canada:** Indeed's Hiring Lab found employment losses for 20 to 24 year olds concentrated in professional and technical services and finance, and postings in tech remain weaker relative to pre-pandemic levels than other fields ([Indeed Hiring Lab, May 2026](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/en-ca/2026/05/06/canadian-youth-labour-market-struggles-continue/)).
- **More applicants per posting (US data):** on Handshake, the main US campus job board, postings fell 15% and applications per job rose 30% over the 2024-25 year ([Handshake, Class of 2025](https://joinhandshake.com/network-trends/class-of-2025-graduation/)). Software engineering dropped from a top-five most-posted entry-level role to ninth ([Handshake, CS spotlight](https://joinhandshake.com/blog/network-trends/class-of-2026-spotlight-computer-science/)).
- **Fewer new-grad hires at big tech:** SignalFire's 2025 report found new grads were 7% of big-tech hires, with new-grad hiring down 25% from 2023 and more than 50% from 2019 ([SignalFire](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025)).

**What that means for you:** apply to more places than students did five years ago, don't skip local, government, and SIP postings because they're less glamorous, and count research positions as real experience. The applications are more automated too: see [Applications and Offers](applications-and-offers.md) for AI screening disclosure rules.

## International Students

You can do SIP and you no longer need a separate co-op work permit for it, and eligible students can work full-time off campus during the summer break without SIP. Conditions apply and some employers require citizenship. The details and sources are on the [SIP page](science-internship-program.md#international-students); confirm your own situation with [ISVS](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/international/international-student-services/contact-us/index.html) before you accept an offer.
