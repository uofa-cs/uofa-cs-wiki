# CMPUT 382: Introduction to GPU Programming

## What Students Say

> Imported from the original course reviews page, which mixed student contributions with summarized Reddit discussion. Tips aren't term-stamped yet; if you've taken this course recently, [add your experience](https://github.com/uofa-cs/uofa-cs-wiki/issues/new?template=course-review.yml).

Not widely discussed on Reddit because not many students take it. The few who have report that labs take the full 3 hours but the course isn't disproportionately harder than other 300-level courses. Some past offerings were disorganized, with setup friction around toolchains.

**Student tips:**
- Background in 229 (computer organization) helps significantly; GPU optimization requires understanding what the hardware is actually doing.
- If 382 isn't running, [CMPUT 481](cmput-481.md) (Parallel and Distributed Systems) is the closest alternative for parallel programming.
