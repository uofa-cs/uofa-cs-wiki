# Courses

Every undergraduate CMPUT course offered since Fall 2024 has a page here. Course facts (title, prerequisites, when it's offered) are synced from the [official catalogue](https://apps.ualberta.ca/catalogue/course/cmput); everything under "What Students Say" comes from students who took it.

**Took a course?** [Add a review](https://github.com/uofa-cs/uofa-cs-wiki/issues/new?template=course-review.yml). It takes five minutes and you don't need git. Courses without reviews are the ones that need you most.

> **About instructors.** This wiki doesn't rank professors. Course pages may describe how specific sections have been run (notes, assessments, pacing) so you can pick a style that suits you. Instructors change every term: check the catalogue for who's teaching before you register.

For how courses fit together, see the [prerequisite maps in Degree Planning](../getting-started/degree-planning.md#prerequisite-map). For which languages and tools each course uses, see the [Curriculum Map](../skills/curriculum-map.md).

Difficulty and workload reflect student consensus, on these scales:

- **Difficulty:** Easy / Medium / Hard / Brutal
- **Workload:** Light / Moderate / Heavy / Overwhelming

## Scheduling Advice From Students

- **201 and 204 pair well** in second year: different kinds of difficulty (201 is mechanical and time-consuming, 204 is abstract).
- **Don't stack 229 with other heavy courses.** Students consistently rate it the hardest course in the program. 229 + 301 + 350 in one term is described as survivable but brutal.
- **415's project is close to a full-time commitment.** Don't take it alongside other project-heavy courses.
- **301 and 404 depend heavily on your team.** Form or screen your team early.
