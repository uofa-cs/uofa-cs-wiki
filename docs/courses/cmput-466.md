# CMPUT 466: Machine Learning Essentials

## What Students Say

> Imported from the original course reviews page, which mixed student contributions with summarized Reddit discussion. Tips aren't term-stamped yet; if you've taken this course recently, [add your experience](https://github.com/uofa-cs/uofa-cs-wiki/issues/new?template=course-review.yml).

Supervised learning, neural networks, probabilistic models, optimization. The experience varies a lot by section, so it's worth asking upper-years about the current instructor.

**Instructor notes:**
- **Dale Schuurmans:** students praise the textbook he uses and describe exams and assignments as fair.
- **Russ Greiner:** enthusiastic lecturer; former students credit his section with launching their interest in ML.

**Student tips:**
- Check who is teaching before registering and ask upper-years how that section has been run (exam format, pacing, practice material).
- CMPUT 267 and MATH 125 are important background. Students who skipped 267 often feel lost on the math.
- 466 is the one-course alternative to the 267 + 467 sequence. If you're in an AI Option or want deeper ML, check whether your program needs 467 instead; you can't get credit for 466 once you have 467.
