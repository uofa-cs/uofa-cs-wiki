# CMPUT 175: Introduction to the Foundations of Computation II

## What Students Say

> Imported from the original course reviews page, which mixed student contributions with summarized Reddit discussion. Tips aren't term-stamped yet; if you've taken this course recently, [add your experience](https://github.com/uofa-cs/uofa-cs-wiki/issues/new?template=course-review.yml).

Continues from 174. Object-oriented programming, more complex data structures, recursion deepened. The jump from 174 to 175 catches a lot of students by surprise.

**Student tips:**
- The object-oriented ideas introduced here (classes, inheritance) come back in [301](cmput-301.md)'s object-oriented design. Don't treat this as just "more Python."
