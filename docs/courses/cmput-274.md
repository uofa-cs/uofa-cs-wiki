# CMPUT 274: Accelerated Introduction to the Foundations of Computation I

## What Students Say

> Imported from the original course reviews page, which mixed student contributions with summarized Reddit discussion. Tips aren't term-stamped yet; if you've taken this course recently, [add your experience](https://github.com/uofa-cs/uofa-cs-wiki/issues/new?template=course-review.yml).

The accelerated alternative to 174/175: 274 is Python on Linux, and [275](cmput-275.md) moves to object-oriented C++. Reviews for both halves live here.

You can't get credit for both 275 and 201, so 275 also covers the role 201 plays in the regular stream.

Students are divided: those with strong prior experience often found it manageable and preferred the depth; those without a strong base often found the pace brutal, with some reporting around 20 hours a week on this one course.

**Student tips:**
- Be honest about your background. If you've programmed seriously before, the accelerated stream is a good fit. Otherwise, 174/175 is the smarter play.
