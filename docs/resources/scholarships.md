# Scholarships and Awards

*Last verified: October 2026.*

The big recurring awards UofA CS students actually get, with every amount and deadline linked to its source. Amounts and dates change; click through before you plan around a number.

---

## Awards Hub: apply every year

[Awards Hub](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/registrar/scholarships-awards-financial-support/undergraduate-awards/index.html) is the Registrar's single application for undergraduate scholarships. One application assesses you for awards based on academics, leadership, equity and need; UofA gives out over $50 million a year this way.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Continuing students** | Apply **February 1 to March 31** (next cycle opens February 2027) |
| **Entering from high school** | October 1 to January 10 |
| **Academic scholarships** | Minimum **3.5 GPA** on at least 24 credits over the most recent September-April |
| **Leadership awards** | Minimum 2.0 GPA on a full-time load |
| **Results** | All applicants notified by the end of September |

Meeting the minimum doesn't guarantee anything, but it's an hour of work. Apply every year.

## Jason Lang and Louise McKinney (Alberta residents)

Funded by the Government of Alberta, applied for through Awards Hub ([Registrar](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/registrar/scholarships-awards-financial-support/undergraduate-awards/jason-lang-louise-mckinney.html), [Alberta Student Aid](https://studentaid.alberta.ca/scholarships/jason-lang-scholarship/)):

| | Jason Lang | Louise McKinney |
|---|---|---|
| **Amount** | $1,000 | $2,500 (only a few available) |
| **Who** | Alberta residents (Canadian citizen, PR, or Protected Person), full-time, second year or later | Same, but transfer students aren't eligible |
| **Grades** | Minimum **3.5 GPA** over the previous September-April, on at least 80% of a full load (24 units for most programs) | Same |
| **Deadlines** | Applications open August 4. Fall deadline **September 15**, winter deadline **January 15** | **Must apply by September 15** |

- You can get only one of the two per year; if you miss Louise McKinney you're automatically considered for Jason Lang.
- Lifetime maximum of three Jason Lang Scholarships.
- The GPA minimum rose from **3.2 to 3.5** starting Fall 2025. This is no longer "decent grades" territory.
- Missed September 15, 2026? You can still apply for Jason Lang by **January 15, 2027**.

## Dean's Honor Roll

Not money, but worth listing on resumes and grad applications. The 2026-27 calendar's [Faculty of Science regulations](https://calendar.ualberta.ca/content.php?catoid=69&navoid=20749) give First Class Standing (the Dean's Honor Roll) to students in Pre-Fall 2024 programs who complete at least 24 units of letter-graded courses in a Fall/Winter with at least a **3.5 GPA** (12 units if you were on Study Abroad or the Science Internship Program for one of those terms). It's a GPA threshold, not a top-X% ranking.

## Paid undergraduate research

All three run through the Undergraduate Research Initiative (URI) with one **February** deadline (February 2027, exact date TBA; last year's was February 2) for projects starting the following Summer, Fall, or Winter. Each needs a confirmed supervisor before you apply ([URI funding page](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/funding/index.html)).

| Award | Value | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [NSERC USRA](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/funding/usra.html) | $6,000 + at least $2,400 supervisor top-up = **at least $8,400** for 16 weeks | Canadian citizens, PRs, Protected Persons; cumulative average normally at least B-; max three over your degree ([NSERC](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/undergraduate-student-research-awards)) |
| [URI Undergraduate Researcher Stipend](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/funding/uri-streamlined-application-detailed-eligibility.html) | **$7,500** for a 4-12 month project | Current UofA undergrads in their first degree, satisfactory standing; GPA not used in adjudication; once per lifetime; no citizenship requirement listed |
| [Alberta Innovates Summer Research Studentship](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/funding/uri-streamlined-application-detailed-eligibility.html) | **$1,875/month** for 2-4 months, May-August | Minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA; health research only (CS fits under "Digital Health + Data-Driven Systems") |

!!! note "URI Stipend amount"
    Older URI documents (such as a 2017 program guide that is still online) list a smaller amount, and the USRA's $6,000 NSERC portion sometimes gets mixed up with it. The current figure on URI's [detailed eligibility page](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/funding/uri-streamlined-application-detailed-eligibility.html) is **$7,500**.

About 40% of streamlined applicants got an offer last cycle ([URI](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/funding/uri-streamlined-application-awardshub.html)). How to find a supervisor and what the timeline looks like: [Getting Into Research](../research/getting-into-research.md).

## Graduate funding (worth knowing early)

- UofA CS guarantees full-time thesis MSc students at least **$30,800** and PhD students at least **$31,800** ([department](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/computing-science/graduate-studies/financial-support-and-awards/index.html)), and the university guarantees PhD students at least **$100,000 over four years** ([GPS](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-studies/fees-funding/scholarships-awards/minimum-guaranteed-funding-for-phd-students.html)).
- National awards: **CGRS-M** is $27,000 for 12 months, due December 1 ([NSERC](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/canada-graduate-research-scholarship-masters-program)); **CGRS-D** is $40,000/year for 36 months ([NSERC](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/canada-graduate-research-scholarship-doctoral-program)).

See [Grad School](../research/grad-school.md).

## Need-based aid

Loans and grants come through [Alberta Student Aid](https://studentaid.alberta.ca/). One application covers provincial and federal funding, including Canada Student Grants, which don't need to be repaid. It stacks with everything above.

## Tips

- **Keep your GPA at 3.5+ if you can.** It's the line for Awards Hub academic scholarships, Jason Lang, Louise McKinney, and the Dean's Honor Roll.
- **Start research conversations in October or November.** Every research award above needs a supervisor before February.
- **Give references and supervisors at least four weeks' notice**, with your resume and the award details.
